We now head to Sir Bani Yas, an island that was made to be a refuge for the wildlife, like the Arabian oryx.

When we met her, one of the hyenas just went out of its place, so she had to shoot it with an anaesthetic to make it sleep, and then to move it delicately back to its original place.
